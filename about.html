HELLO, I'M

Dhairyash Kotwani

Competitive Cyclist

I started India Racing Cycles to make it easier for riders in India to discover races, competitions, cyclothons, and cycling news in one place. I wanted to bring useful event information together because announcements are often spread across different websites, clubs, and social media pages.

Why I built this website

Finding a race should not mean checking dozens of pages and hoping you have not missed an announcement. India Racing Cycles is an independent community calendar designed to make event discovery simpler.

The aim is simple: discover events, share useful information, and help more people get to the starting line. Event details should always be checked with the original organiser before making travel or payment plans.

pedal_bike My cycling journey

I have been involved in competitive road cycling since around 2012–2013. Racing has taught me discipline, consistency, patience, and how much a supportive cycling community matters.

  • Amateur Individual Time Trial win in Bengaluru, January 2026.
  • Fifth place at the Nandi Race in November 2025.
  • Top-three finishes in road races in 2017 and 2018.

security My professional journey

My professional background is in IT Audit, Technology Risk and controls assurance. I have worked with teams at EY India, Goldman Sachs, and KPMG Global Services.

I am interested in how organisations protect systems, manage technology risk, and build reliable security controls.

school Learning and education

I hold a Bachelor of Engineering in Electronics & Telecommunication. I am also pursuing an M.Sc. in Computer Science with a focus on Cyber Security at SRH University of Applied Sciences in Leipzig, Germany.

My current learning interests include Ethical hacking, penetration testing, security technologies, cloud security, and information security management.

interests Beyond cycling and work

I enjoy learning about cybersecurity, technology, ethical hacking, and new tools. I also like building small digital projects and finding practical ways to make information easier to use.

India Racing Cycles is an independent community project. It is not an official website of the Cycling Federation of India or any event organiser. Event information is shared with source links wherever possible and should be confirmed with the organiser.
